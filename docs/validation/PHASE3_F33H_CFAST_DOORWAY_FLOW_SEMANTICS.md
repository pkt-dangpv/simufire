# Phase 3+ F3.3h - CFAST doorway-flow source-to-output semantics

Date: 2026-07-22

## Decision

**Diagnostic GO. The CFAST comparison remains valid, but the F3.3f1
source-preserving destination hypothesis is rejected.**

CFAST assigns three different owners to horizontal doorway transport:

1. source-zone removal is selected geometrically from slab height;
2. direct destination deposition is split by source-slab temperature versus
   receiver upper/lower temperatures;
3. Poreh entrainment is a separate receiver-internal upper/lower transfer.

The `.out` upper/lower vent table and spreadsheet `Total Inflow/Outflow`
measure owner 1/2 only. They read `ventptr%h_mflow`, which is populated by
`flogo`. They do not include `uflw3` or `h_mflow_mix`. Poreh is nevertheless
physical: `wall_flow` adds `uflw3` to `uflw_hf`, which enters the ODE through
`flows_hvents`.

Therefore the Group C direct-flow targets used since F3.3f are comparable to
SimuFire's direct accepted opening/pressure ledgers. The mistake was not the
measurement convention; it was assuming that CFAST preserves the source zone
at the receiver.

No SimuFire motor, runner, case, report, baseline, tolerance, CTRL or gap was
changed in this phase.

## End-to-end owner map

### 1. Slab generation

`wall_flow()` calls `ventw()` to obtain, per slab:

- direction `dirs12`;
- absolute height `yslab`;
- mass-flow magnitude `xmslab`;
- source temperature `tslab`;
- enthalpy and species payloads.

### 2. Direct transport: `flogo` / `uflw2`

Origin removal is geometric:

```text
if yslab >= source_interface:
    remove from source upper
else:
    remove from source lower
```

Destination deposition is thermal, not geometric and not source-preserving:

```text
Tlower_eff = min(Tlower_receiver, Tupper_receiver)
f_upper = tanhsmooth(
    T_slab,
    Tupper_receiver + 1 K,
    Tlower_eff - 1 K,
    1,
    0
)
f_lower = 1 - f_upper
```

The current CFAST `tanhsmooth` is:

```text
f = clamp(
    0.5 + 0.5025 * tanh(6 / (xmax-xmin) * (x-xmin) - 3),
    0,
    1
)
```

Mass, enthalpy, O2 and every species are split by the same `f_upper/f_lower`.
`flogo` writes both the ODE contribution `uflw2` and the direct output matrix
`ventptr%h_mflow`.

### 3. Receiver mixing: `spill_plume` / `uflw3`

When a flowing slab lies on opposite sides of the source and receiver
interfaces, `spill_plume` calculates Poreh mixing:

- hot source-upper into receiver-lower geometry: receiver `lower -> upper`;
- cool source-lower into receiver-upper geometry: receiver `upper -> lower`,
  multiplied by `0.25`.

This transfer uses receiver composition and has zero net room mass. It is
stored in `uflw3`; `h_mflow_mix` receives its mass values for internal state.

### 4. ODE accumulation

`wall_flow` performs:

```text
uflw_hf = uflw2 + uflw3
```

`solve.f90` then adds `flows_hvents` to `flows_total` for upper and lower
mass, energy and species. Poreh is therefore part of layer evolution.

### 5. Published flow output

`results_vent_flows()` passes only `ventptr%h_mflow` to `flwout()`.
Spreadsheet outputs `Net Inflow` and `Total Inflow/Outflow Upper/Lower` also
read only `h_mflow`.

Consequences:

- published upper/lower vent flows are direct `flogo` transport;
- they include the temperature-based receiver split;
- they exclude receiver-internal Poreh mixing;
- the ODE includes both direct transport and Poreh mixing.

`h_mflow_mix` is not referenced by the standard output writers.

## Group C comparison in one convention

The existing CFAST integrals from `cfast_corridor_chain.out` are direct
`h_mflow` values. The SimuFire comparison uses accepted F3.3a opening plus
F3.3b pressure routes, also direct transport. Neither side adds Poreh mixing
to the reported direct inflow.

| Window | CFAST direct lower in | CFAST direct upper in | SimuFire direct lower in | SimuFire direct upper in |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0-180 s | 65.782 kg | 3.662 kg | 63.196 kg | 18.472 kg |
| 180-300 s | 66.520 kg | 1.825 kg | 54.918 kg | 34.452 kg |
| 300-590 s | 166.815 kg | 1.578 kg | 51.599 kg | 171.890 kg |

At 180 s, the CFAST `.out` instantaneous R0 inflow is
`0.543 kg/s` lower and `0.02465 kg/s` upper. That split is the result of
`flogo` buoyancy routing. It is not evidence that the incoming Hall upper
stream remains upper in R0.

The late mismatch diagnosed in F3.3f is therefore real: SimuFire increasingly
deposits cool incoming gas into upper based on geometric midpoint, while
CFAST deposits nearly all of it into lower based on temperature. However,
the selected F3.3f1 remedy was wrong: source-preserving routing also sends too
much Hall upper gas to R0 upper and removes the CFAST buoyancy split.

## Explanation of F3.3f2 and F3.3g1

F3.3f2 failed because source identity is not CFAST's receiver rule. It reduced
upper direct inflow to almost zero in the tested canonical state rather than
reproducing the smooth thermal split.

F3.3g1 correctly added the separate Poreh owner, but retained the wrong
source-preserving direct owner. The hot mixing branch (`0.036 kg`) was far too
small to reconstruct the missing direct upper fraction, while the cool branch
(`0.738 kg`) moved receiver upper inventory downward. Exact conservation
could not repair the incorrect direct destination semantics.

## Next gate: F3.3h1 pure buoyancy routing

F3.3h1 may implement only a pure/default-false route selector matching
`flogo`:

1. preserve geometric source-zone removal;
2. calculate `f_upper/f_lower` from slab source temperature and receiver
   pre-step upper/lower temperatures;
3. split one direct slab into two atomic routes with identical mass,
   enthalpy, O2 and species fractions;
4. preserve gross flow, pressure integration, neutral plane and source
   payload exactly;
5. keep Poreh as a separate optional receiver-internal bundle;
6. expose no Engine, CLI, CSV or case flag in the design phase.

Required fixtures:

- exact CFAST `tanhsmooth` values at lower, middle and upper limits;
- cool slab mostly to lower and hot slab mostly to upper;
- equal receiver temperatures and inverted legacy temperatures;
- split payload conservation for mass, enthalpy, O2 and all species;
- F3.3a and relaxed F3.3b slab equivalence;
- opening-order independence;
- exact default-OFF equivalence;
- paired direct plus Poreh bundles remain separately identifiable.

Only after that fixture gate may F3.3h2 temporarily expose a default-OFF
runtime candidate and stop at 180 s. It must compare direct output to CFAST
`h_mflow` and compare total zone evolution to `uflw2 + uflw3`; those are two
different acceptance tables.

## STOP conditions

Stop before runtime if the candidate:

- chooses destination by height or source identity;
- uses receiver temperature to rewrite the source payload;
- combines direct mass and Poreh mass into one published flow quantity;
- changes pressure, opening coefficient or total slab flow;
- uses different fractions for mass, energy, O2 or species;
- requires a per-case coefficient;
- changes legacy behavior while default OFF.

Do not reintroduce F3.3e1 Qc, run 300/590 s, edit official reports, alter
baselines/tolerances/gaps, promote canonical authority or touch HVAC in
F3.3h1.
